Privacy Notice

TaskKin Care is a family care coordination and information-organizing tool. It is not a medical device, medical service, or emergency-response service.

TaskKin Care is developed and operated by Jun Yu, an independent developer.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address and authentication credentials. TaskKin Care does not store plaintext passwords.
  • Family and member information: household name, care-recipient label, member name, relationship, role, time zone, and availability.
  • Coordination content: tasks, timeline events, document metadata, notification preferences, reports, and audit records.
  • Files you choose to upload: content and metadata for files used in family coordination. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device; file contents are not sent to any external service for OCR.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: when you subscribe to Family Plus, payment is processed by Apple through your Apple ID; we do not receive or store your payment card or Apple account credentials. We store subscription metadata needed to provide access (Apple transaction identifier, plan, and renewal/expiry date) linked to your account and household.

2. Non-PHI use

TaskKin Care does not ask for and must not be used to submit diagnoses, prescriptions, insurance, payment, precise location, or other protected health information (PHI). It does not currently include automatic PHI detection or redaction. Please remove or avoid PHI before uploading a file.

3. How we use information

  • Provide family coordination features and role-based access.
  • Show in-app role-aware coordination updates and generate shareable weekly summaries.
  • Maintain an audit trail for key coordination writes, such as tasks, role changes, and report generation.
  • Maintain reliability and security.

4. Sharing and service providers

  • Household members may access only the family information their role permits.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We use Supabase for authentication, database, object storage, and realtime synchronization. TaskKin Care does not send uploaded content to external AI or OCR providers.

5. Security

  • Data is transmitted through encrypted connections supported by our service providers.
  • Row-level security separates households and role-based access limits what members can access.
  • Key coordination writes are recorded in append-only audit records.

6. Retention, deletion, and your requests

You may delete your account and household data in the app through Settings > Delete account & household data. You may request access, correction, export, or other privacy assistance by emailing Billy.yu@me.com. Audit records are retained for 24 months after household deletion for security and accountability.

7. California Privacy Notice and Notice at Collection

This section applies only where the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act apply to TaskKin Care. We collect the categories described in Section 1 for the purposes described in Section 3. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law.

At or before collection, California users receive this notice describing the categories of personal information collected, the purposes for collection, and the retention and deletion request process. We do not collect or use sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit under California law.

California residents may request to know, correct, or delete eligible personal information, and may use an authorized agent where permitted by law. Send requests to Billy.yu@me.com; we may request information needed to verify the request and will respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Children, changes, and contact

TaskKin Care is for adults and authorized representatives, not child-directed accounts. We will describe material changes when this notice is updated. For privacy questions or requests, email Billy.yu@me.com.

Effective: July 26, 2026. Operator-reviewed product notice; independent legal counsel review has not been obtained.
