Why TaskKin Care

Family care often leaves one person maintaining every update, following up, and doing the work. TaskKin Care makes information actionable and makes ownership visible.

01

Action before record

Every task has an owner, due time, and status instead of becoming another message in a group chat.

02

Honest handoffs

Family members can claim, decline, or hand off a task so responsibilities remain visible.

03

Calm notifications

Updates are shown by role and priority to keep people informed without creating noise.

Frequently asked questions

Is TaskKin Care a medical service?

No. It is a family coordination tool. It does not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, clinical advice, or emergency services.

Can I upload health records?

No. TaskKin Care is not intended for PHI. Do not upload diagnoses, prescriptions, insurance information, or other PHI.

Who can see a task?

Role-based access controls limit data visibility. Viewers do not have API access to tasks, documents, or audit records.

What languages are supported?

The app and these pages are available in English, Chinese, and Spanish.
