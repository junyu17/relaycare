8) IAP
Family Plus is an optional auto-renewable household subscription, purchasable/restorable only by the Coordinator:
- Monthly: `TaskKin.care.pro.mon`, 1 month, StoreKit-localized price.
- Yearly: `TaskKin.care.pro.yearly`, 1 year, StoreKit-localized price.

Plus unlocks 3 households/12 members, unlimited active tasks, 50 OCR uploads/month, PDF/CSV export, advanced notifications, weekly report history, and 3-year audit retention. Path: Settings > Plan > Upgrade to Family Plus. Paywall shows duration, localized price, renewal terms, Restore, Terms, and Privacy. Manage Plan opens Apple subscription settings. Account deletion does not cancel the subscription.

Account deletion (Settings > Delete account & households) permanently deletes the account and every household the user coordinates, including all data and audit. Memberships in other households are anonymized as "Deleted member"; their shared records remain.
