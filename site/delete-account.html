Delete your TaskKin Care account

You can delete your account and all associated household data directly in the app:

  1. Open TaskKin Care on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account, household, and all household data (tasks, timeline, documents) are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

Alternative request (outside the app)

If you cannot sign in to the app, you may request account deletion by email. Send a request from the email address registered to your account to:

Billy.yu@me.com

Include the subject line "Account deletion request". We process verified deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your user account, all memberships in households you created, uploaded documents (including OCR text), tasks, timeline events, and subscription entitlement records. Audit records are retained for 24 months after deletion for security and accountability (see the Privacy Policy).

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription automatically. Please cancel separately: iOS – Apple subscription settings; Android – Google Play subscription settings.
