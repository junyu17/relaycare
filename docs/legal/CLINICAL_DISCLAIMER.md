# Clinical Disclaimer & Emergency Guidance (DRAFT)

> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — 2026-07-24. Requires review by a clinical content advisor and US medical legal counsel before production use.

## 1. Not a Medical Service

RelayCare is a **family care coordination and information-organization tool**. It is not:

- A medical device or diagnostic tool.
- A telehealth or medical advice service.
- A prescription, dosage, or medication management service.
- An emergency or triage service.
- A substitute for physicians, nurses, pharmacists, caregivers, or emergency responders.

RelayCare does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatments, interpret symptoms, or make clinical decisions.

## 2. Emergency Guidance

**In a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately (911 in the US) or go to the nearest emergency facility. Do not rely on RelayCare for urgent situations.**

RelayCare tasks, reminders, and notifications are coordination aids. A missed or delayed notification must never be treated as clinical guidance. If you are unsure whether a situation is urgent, contact a qualified medical professional or emergency services.

## 3. Information Accuracy

Care timelines, tasks, and document metadata reflect what family members entered. They are not verified medical records and may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Always confirm medical instructions (appointments, medications, follow-ups) directly with the relevant clinician.

## 4. Documents and OCR/AI

Uploaded documents are shared for family coordination. OCR-extracted candidate fields and AI-generated summaries are **assistive only**, carry a confidence indicator, and require human confirmation before any action is taken. They may contain errors. See `AI_OCR_NOTICE.md`. Low-confidence fields are never automatically written to any record and never automatically create high-risk tasks.

## 5. Third-Party Information

Appointment details, instructions, or documents originating from clinics, pharmacies, or other third parties may be mis-transcribed or outdated when entered into RelayCare. Verify with the original source before acting.

## 6. Boundary Statement (MVP Scope)

The MVP explicitly excludes diagnosis, prescription, billing, insurance, emergency triage, deep EMR/EHR integration, and automatic PHI processing. Any expansion beyond this boundary requires separate legal and clinical review and approval.
