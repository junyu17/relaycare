Delete your TaskKin Care account

You can delete your account and its household data directly in the app:

  1. Open TaskKin Care on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account & households. If you have not created or joined a family yet, the same Delete account & households link is at the bottom of the Set up your care circle screen.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you confirm with Apple once more so that we can ask Apple to revoke TaskKin Care's access to your Apple ID. Your account is permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

If Apple cannot be reached at that moment, your account is still deleted. You can check or remove TaskKin Care yourself on your iPhone under Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

What gets deleted

Your user account is permanently deleted. Every household you coordinate is permanently deleted together with all of its data: tasks, timeline events, uploaded documents (including OCR text), reports, and audit records.

If you also belong to other households that you do not coordinate, your membership is anonymized. Your name is replaced with a "Deleted member" placeholder, so shared tasks, documents, and audit records you created remain in those households and stay attributable without retaining your account link or profile name. Those households are not deleted.

Accounts you no longer use

If your account is not linked to Sign in with Apple or to an email and password, nobody else is in your family, and the account has never had a subscription, it is deleted automatically, with its data and uploaded files, after 180 days without use (30 days if it never created or joined a family). Accounts in a family that other members still use are never deleted automatically. See the Privacy Notice for details.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription automatically. Please cancel separately: iOS – Apple subscription settings; Android – Google Play subscription settings.

Alternative request (outside the app)

If you cannot use the app, you may request account deletion by email to:

Billy.yu@me.com

Include the subject line "Account deletion request". We process verified deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email. How we verify the request depends on how you signed in:

  • Email and password: send the request from the email address registered to your account.
  • Sign in with Apple: the quickest way is to open TaskKin Care on any iPhone, tap Continue with Apple, and delete the account in the app. If that is not possible, email us the Account ID shown in Settings → Account and we will reply to arrange verification.
  • No sign-in (you chose Start without an account or joined with a family code): include the Account ID shown in Settings → Account. If you can no longer open the app, tell us the family's name, your display name in that family, and roughly when you created it. To protect families, we delete an account on an email request only if it has no Apple or email sign-in, it matches all of these details, nobody else is in its family, and it has not been used in the last 14 days. If other people are in that family, we will reply to arrange the request with the family's coordinator.
